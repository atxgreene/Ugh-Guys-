Terrain — Rivers, Fords & Roads

Carved Waterways

Rivers meander-walk from source to basin, carving the heightfield as they go. A water mesh sits in the channel with sky-tinted sheen. Crossing slows units to 0.45× — unless they find a ford (0.85×). Ancient roads connect ruins and fords, granting 1.28× speed to any unit on the path. Each tile's speed modifier feeds directly into the pathfinder's cost function.

River: 0.45× speed
Ford: 0.85× speed
Road: 1.28× speed

Atmosphere — God-Rays & Cloud Shadows

🌅 God-Rays

Light Shafts

Additive plane-slabs fan from the key sun across the map. Shimmer-animated via UV scroll. Opacity scales with the time-of-day mood — storm and dusk make them dramatic. Lightning frames flare them white.

☁ Cloud Shadows

Drifting Darkness

A canvas-generated cloud texture scrolls over a large plane above the ground. Soft-edged blobs of shadow drift with the storm — mood-scaled opacity, always moving.

Performance — Instancing & LOD

Geometry Merging Static doodads (obelisks, bones, tablets, trees) batched per material bucket using mergeGeometries() — one draw call per material type.
Shader-Baked Fog Merged batches inject per-fragment fog via onBeforeCompile. Fog of war applied to the batch group, not per-doodad.
Shadow Pass Skip Merged clutter geometry casts no shadows — saves an entire depth pass on dense maps.
Result Hundreds of draw calls on a Huge map collapsed to a handful of batch draws. Significant frame-time savings on mid-range GPUs.
