In-Game Secrets

These activate during a match, after you've chosen your faction.

Trigger What Happens
greene Spawns the Fields of Evil immediately — the House of Greene, Landry's and Conner's clans feuding around it, the hounds at the door, and the Warwagon. Normally only an 18% chance per map. Type any time during play.
Find the House Discovery toast fires + Mr Greene's portrait dialogue appears at the bottom of the HUD. "Welcome to my Fields, stranger. The hounds caught your scent a mile off."
First Landonian sighted Landry · King of the Landonians portrait pops up. "You're standing in my Fields. Bold move — I respect it."
First Boydonian sighted Conner · King of the Boydonians portrait pops up. "Swing true, fear nothing. This is Boydonian ground."
First sight of Parker Parker the Shepherd greets you — then checks in periodically while he's in view. "Oh — hello there! Parker's the name. How are you holding up?"
First sight of Tucker Tucker the Goldendoodle sniffs you thoroughly, wags once, and trots back to the house. Verdict: you passed.
First sight of Gatsby Gatsby the French Bulldog stares at you with absolute confidence. He has never doubted himself. Not once.
Raze the House of Greene +500 Forbidden Knowledge  ·  +200 Divine Favor Toast: "☩ The House of Greene has fallen. Their hoard of forbidden knowledge is yours."
Portraits appear in the bronze-bordered dialogue box at bottom-centre of the HUD. Click it (or wait 8 s) to dismiss.
