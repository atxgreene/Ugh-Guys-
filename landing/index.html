The Setting

The World Before the Flood

Humanity reached impossible heights — metallurgy, astronomy, monumental architecture, and ritual knowledge — but was corrupted by the forbidden teachings the Watchers brought down from heaven. Cities of gold rise beside obsidian towers of star-metal, while the giants gather in the mountains. The sky remembers. The waters are coming.

Three powers. One doomed age. Choose your fate before the Flood.
Three Asymmetric Powers

Choose Your People

Each faction is genuinely different — its own units, buildings, upgrade trees, economy, and path to victory.

The Covenant Cities

Build. Defend. Endure.

Priest-king city-states bound by faith, law, and the covenant. Bronze, grain, and the favor of heaven hold their walls against the encroaching dark.

  • Balanced economy & strong defenses
  • Disciplined bronze infantry & chariots
  • Temple favor, prophets, holy fire

The Watcher Remnant

Forbidden Knowledge. Rewrite Creation.

Fallen sons of heaven and their bound disciples. They wield star-metal, burning script, and hybrid flesh to transcend mortal law — and corrupt the world.

  • Powerful elite units & deep tech
  • Devastating sigil-casters & skyfire
  • Risk-reward forbidden power

The Nephilim Clans

Break. Consume. Dominate.

Giant-blooded clans of the burning mountains. They do not build walls — they break them, with brutal raids, war-beasts, and giants older than the cities they end.

  • Overwhelming melee & fast raids
  • Mountain giants that level bases
  • Fear, fury, and bone totems
A Complete RTS, Built From Scratch

No Engine. No Assets. Just Code.

Every model, sound, texture, and map is generated procedurally in the browser with Three.js — no game engine, no art files, no copyrighted IP.

⚔️

Real RTS Loop

Gather, build, tech up, raise an army, and crush the enemy's seat of power. Worker harassment and expansion matter.

🧠

Reactive AI

An opponent that manages workers, follows build orders, scouts, defends, and escalates into coordinated attack waves.

🌫️

Fog of War

Persistent discovery with real-time visibility, minimap intel, and neutral guardians watching the obelisks.

🗺️

Six Living Biomes

River valley, cedar stands, basalt highlands, Nephilim wasteland, Watcher ruins, sacred foothills — each with its own mood sky.

🎬

Cinematic Presentation

Opening flyover, faction intro cards, bloom-lit sacred fire, storm-lightning skies, and an end-game camera over the ruins.

🏛️

Distinct Factions

Detailed, readable unit silhouettes; buildings that rise on scaffolds and burn as they fall; counter-based combat.
